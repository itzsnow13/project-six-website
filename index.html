Order of battle

Divisions

Six divisions and Command under one hexagon. Pick a home division when you enlist, then fly with any of them on org nights. Each crest lights its own segment of the mark; Command lights them all.

How it works

From recruit to crew

  1. 01

    Enlist

    Sign in with Discord, verify your RSI profile and send your application from the Enlist page.

  2. 02

    Get approved

    A division lead reviews it, usually within 72 hours. You can follow every step in the member portal.

  3. 03

    Join a division

    Finish onboarding in Discord with a supervisor, and your division sets you up with roles.

  4. 04

    Take work orders

    Join jobs that need crew from the member portal, or post your own for your division.

  5. 05

    Earn & advance

    Earn org rewards, share profit with the org if you choose, and build your standing.
